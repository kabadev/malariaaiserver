On-Device TFLite Engine

Runs TensorFlow Lite C++ binary models locally on phone hardware without needing an internet connection or cellular coverage.

Automated Outbreak Monitoring

Backend scheduled engine continuously aggregates regional case densities every 15 minutes, triggering emergency SMS & Email alerts when thresholds are reached.

Scoped Patient Metadata

Attaches patient name, age, gender, unique patient ID, and parasite counts (parasites/μL) to every diagnostic scan record.
