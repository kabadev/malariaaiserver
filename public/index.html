Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about implementing Malaria AI in your health center

Does this app require an active internet connection?

No. The core AI neural network (`malaria_classifier.tflite`) runs completely on-device. Diagnostic scans and patient logging work 100% offline in remote areas without cellular service.

What microscope attachments can we use?

You can use low-cost clip-on smartphone microscope lenses (100x–1000x magnification), Foldscopes, CellScope adapters, or simply capture photos directly through a lab microscope eyepiece.

How are patient data and privacy protected?

All local patient data is stored in encrypted SQLite databases on the phone. Cloud sync uses multi-tenant JWT authentication, ensuring records are strictly scoped to your clinic account.

How does the outbreak alerting system work?

When field workers sync data, our surveillance engine aggregates case numbers by district. If positive cases exceed your threshold (e.g. 10 cases in 24h), SMS/Email alerts are dispatched automatically.
